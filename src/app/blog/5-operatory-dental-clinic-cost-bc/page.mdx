# 5-Operatory Dental Clinic Cost in BC: A 2026 Line-Item Breakdown

Five operatories is the sweet spot for a lot of BC dental practices — big enough to run an associate or two, small enough to still feel manageable. It is also the size we get asked about most often, because it is specific enough that a generic cost-per-square-foot number does not tell you much. Below is the actual line-item breakdown we use when scoping this exact size of clinic.

> **Key takeaway.** A 5-operatory dental clinic in BC typically costs **$660,000 to $1,170,000 in construction** on a **2,200–2,600 sq ft** footprint, working out to roughly **$130,000 to $235,000 per operatory** once you include plumbing, electrical, casework and a proportional share of reception, sterilization and staff areas. Dental equipment for five chairs is a separate budget of roughly $225,000–$450,000.

These totals are the $300–$450 per square foot band from our [dental clinic construction](/services/dental-clinic-construction/) page applied to this footprint. They are not a different number for the same work.

## Cost Per Operatory: What Is Actually in That Number

When clients ask what one operatory costs, the honest answer is that it is never just the room. It includes a fair share of the plumbing trunk lines, shared HVAC capacity, and the central systems serving all five chairs:

1. **Per-operatory rough-in (plumbing + electrical):** $13,000 – $22,000 per chair
2. **Per-operatory casework & finishes:** $16,000 – $29,000 per chair
3. **Shared systems allocation (HVAC, compressor, sterilization):** $27,000 – $45,000 per chair
4. **Shared space allocation (reception, corridors, staff areas):** $33,000 – $52,000 per chair

Add those up and you land at the $130,000–$235,000 per-operatory range above — which is why a quote promising a flat "$80K per chair" is usually excluding shared infrastructure that has to be paid for somewhere.

## How This Compares to Other Clinic Sizes

A 5-operatory clinic sits between a small starter practice and a large multi-operatory facility:

| Clinic size | Operatories | Typical construction cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small — 1,500 sq ft | 2–3 | $450,000 – $675,000 |
| **Mid-size — this guide** | **5** | **$660,000 – $1,170,000** |
| Large — 4,000 sq ft | 7+ | $1,200,000 – $1,800,000 |

If you are still deciding between a 4-operatory and a 6-operatory layout, it is worth speaking with a contractor before finalising floor plans. One or two extra chairs changes plumbing trunk sizing and HVAC capacity, not just square footage.
